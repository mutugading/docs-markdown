## How far the test went

This ran on production, so it stopped short of anything that reaches the accounts:

- One provision and one settlement were created, submitted, revoked and deleted.
- **Confirm was never pressed.** No journal or payment voucher was posted.
- The "From Provision" settlement path was tested **without saving**: saving a draft bill
  against a real provision marks its lines as billed, which would hide them from everyone
  else's picker while the test ran. The saved test settlement used the **Direct** source instead.

Every test value was marked `TEST-CLAUDE`, and the remark on both was
"TEST - Claude, delete me".

## What worked

**Provision**

- Save, and the validation messages for missing or wrong fields (AJU No. must be exactly
  14 characters; PIB No. at most 6).
- Submit is refused until the document has at least one container and one PO, and the
  detail page says so in plain words.
- Draft → Submitted → Revoke (with remark) → Amended → Delete. After submitting, Edit and
  Delete are replaced by Revoke and Confirm, as they should be.
- The print PDF renders.
- LC (USD) is FC ÷ exchange rate. Checked against 2026001008: IDR 2,750,000 ÷ 17,594 = 156.30.

**Provision → settlement handoff (not saved)**

- "Choose provision" lists 232 unbilled invoices, with one-provision and across-a-vendor
  modes and paging.
- Picking the SINAR TIRTA EMAS invoice on 2026000446 copied in the direction, cost type,
  container (40 HFCL), delivery note (EDN-2026000495) and all 5 cost lines. Billed equals
  Provisioned, variance 0.00, total IDR 4,249,000 / USD 240.01, matching the picker.

**Settlement (Direct)**

- Save, then Submit → Revoke → Delete.
- LC = 1,000 ÷ 17,594 = 0.06.
- The line is flagged "Not provisioned" with a variance of −1,000.
- The delete dialog states that any provision lines the settlement claimed are offered for
  settlement again.

## Notes for whoever tests next

- The PO picker filters on the document's **supplier** even when a full PO number is typed.
  A made-up supplier finds nothing; use a real one (IS00295 matches IPO-2026000358). The
  settlement form has no supplier field, so there only a full PO number finds anything.
- The vendor picker's search box does not take focus when it opens; click it before typing.
- After a vendor is picked on an invoice, the tab label changes and the layout shifts,
  so a field typed straight afterwards can land in the wrong place.
- To go past Submit, test on the dev database (192.168.0.7), not here. Confirm posts real
  vouchers, and Finance would have to reverse them.
